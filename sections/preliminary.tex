\section{Preliminaries}

\subsection{Problem Formulation}

We study mixed-autonomy dynamic routing: connected and autonomous vehicles
(CAVs) and human-driven vehicles (HDVs) travel over a directed road network
under time-varying departure demand, and a controller routes the CAVs.  We
first define the problem components and then cast the control process as a
Markov decision process.
% 中文注释：本节定义 mixed-autonomy dynamic routing 问题：在有向路网和时变出发需求下，控制器为 CAV 选路，HDV 不可控。先定义组成部分，再表述为 MDP。

\noindent\textbf{Definition 1 (Segment-Node Road Network).}
The road network is a directed graph \(G=(N,E)\), where each node \(u\in N\)
is a road segment with static attributes (length, speed limit, lane count,
road type, capacity, free-flow travel time), and a directed edge \((u,v)\in E\)
exists when vehicles can move directly from segment \(u\) to segment \(v\).  A
fixed node ordering assigns every segment a stable index and stores the
adjacency matrix \(A\).
% 中文注释：road network 表示为 directed graph \(G=(N,E)\)：node 是 road segment（带静态属性），有向边表示可直接通行；固定 node ordering 存储邻接矩阵 \(A\)。

\noindent\textbf{Definition 2 (Vehicles).}
The departure horizon is partitioned into fixed decision intervals indexed by
decision epochs \(t=0,\ldots,T\).  At epoch \(t\), the active vehicle
population is \(\mathcal{N}_t=\mathcal{N}^{\mathrm{CAV}}_t\cup
\mathcal{N}^{\mathrm{HDV}}_t\).  Each vehicle \(i\) has an origin segment
\(o_i\), a destination segment \(g_i\), a scheduled departure time
\(\tau_i^{\mathrm{dep}}\), and a type \(m_i\in\{\mathrm{CAV},\mathrm{HDV}\}\);
\(u_{i,t}\) denotes its current segment when active.  CAVs are controlled by
the learned routing policy, whereas HDVs are self-interested and cannot be
rerouted.
% 中文注释：出发时域划分为 decision epochs。每辆车有 origin、destination、departure time 和类型；CAV 由策略控制，HDV 自利不可控。

\noindent\textbf{Definition 3 (Route Candidates and Decision Timing).}
An active CAV \(i\) at epoch \(t\) selects one route from its candidates
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{P}_{i,t}
=
\{P^1_{i,t},\ldots,P^{K}_{i,t}\},
\end{equation}
where each candidate is a sequence of segment nodes connecting the current
segment \(u_{i,t}\) to the destination \(g_i\), and the action is the route
index \(a_{i,t}\in\{1,\ldots,K\}\).  Candidates are generated by repeated
Dijkstra search with a route-diversity penalty
(Appendix~\ref{app:math-details}).  An HDV instead makes its route choice
only at its departure time \(\tau_i^{\mathrm{dep}}\).
% 中文注释：active CAV 每轮从 K 条候选（当前 segment 到目的地的 segment 序列）中选一条，action 是 route index；候选由带多样性惩罚的重复 Dijkstra 生成（附录）。HDV 只在出发时选路。

\subsection{Markov Decision Process}

We model the route-choice process as a Markov decision process
\(\mathcal{M}=(\mathcal{S},\mathcal{A},\mathcal{T},r,\gamma)\).
% 中文注释：将选路过程建模为 MDP \(\mathcal{M}=(\mathcal{S},\mathcal{A},\mathcal{T},r,\gamma)\)。

\noindent\textbf{Definition 4 (Observation/State).}
At epoch \(t\), the global state \(s_t\in\mathcal{S}\) includes the road
graph, the scheduled vehicles with their attributes, and the current route
candidates.  Each active CAV observes a projection
\(o_{i,t}=\mathcal{O}_i(s_t)\) containing its current position, destination,
candidate routes, and route-level traffic summaries.  For traffic prediction,
the state is also projected onto the segment graph as
\(X_t=\chi(s_t,G)\), where each segment carries static and dynamic features:
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{l}
X_t(u)=
[\mathrm{c}_t(u),\ v_t(u),
\kappa(u),
\ell(u),\ v_{\max}(u)],
\end{array}
\end{equation}
with vehicle count \(\mathrm{c}_t\), speed \(v_t\), capacity \(\kappa\),
length \(\ell\), and speed limit \(v_{\max}\).

The tensor \(X_t\) is not equivalent to \(s_t\).  It is a lossy
segment-aligned view that removes vehicle identities, individual destinations,
and chosen route suffixes.  These components re-enter the model through the
vehicle observations \(o_{i,t}\), the planned CAV pressure, the departure
demand and the HDV influence, which together condition
the learned transition.
% 中文注释：global state 包含路网、计划车辆及其属性、当前候选路线；active CAV 观察投影 \(o_{i,t}=\mathcal{O}_i(s_t)\)。用于交通预测的 segment 图投影 \(X_t=\chi(s_t,G)\) 是 \(s_t\) 的有损视图，移除的 identity/destination/route suffix 经 \(o_{i,t}\)、CAV pressure、departure demand、HDV influence 和 episode context 重新进入模型。

\noindent\textbf{Definition 5 (Action).}
Let \(\mathcal{I}_t\subseteq\mathcal{N}^{\mathrm{CAV}}_t\) denote the active
CAVs at epoch \(t\).  The joint action is
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{rcl}
a_t&=&(a_{i,t})_{i\in\mathcal{I}_t},
\end{array}
\end{equation}
where \(a_{i,t}=k\) selects candidate route \(P^k_{i,t}\).  HDV route choices
are generated by the background human-routing behavior and are treated as part
of the environment.
% 中文注释：joint action 是 active CAV 的 route index 向量；HDV 选路属于环境。

\noindent\textbf{Definition 6 (Transition).}
Given the current state, the joint CAV action, the departure demand \(D_t\),
and the HDV traffic state \(H_t^{\mathrm{HDV}}\), the transition is
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{l}
s_{t+1}\sim
\mathcal{T}(\cdot\mid s_t,a_t,D_t,H_t^{\mathrm{HDV}}).
\end{array}
\end{equation}
It injects newly departing vehicles, applies the selected routes, advances all
vehicles, and updates segment speeds, occupancies, and active sets.
% 中文注释：transition 注入新出发车辆、应用所选路线、推进所有车辆并更新 segment 速度、占有率和 active 集合。

\noindent\textbf{Definition 7 (Objective/Reward).}
Each completed vehicle \(i\) (CAV or HDV) has travel time
\(\Delta_i=t_i^{\mathrm{arr}}-\tau_i^{\mathrm{dep}}\).  The objective is
system-level: minimize the expected global mean travel time over all completed
vehicles,
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{rcl}
\min\quad
\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{all}}&=&
\displaystyle
{E}_{\pi_\theta,P}
\left[
\frac{1}{|\mathcal{N}^{\mathrm{arr}}|}
\sum_{i\in\mathcal{N}^{\mathrm{arr}}}
\Delta_i
\right],
\end{array}
\end{equation}
where \(\mathcal{N}^{\mathrm{arr}}\) is the set of arrived vehicles.
% 中文注释：目标是最小化所有完成车辆的期望平均旅行时间 \(\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{all}}\)。

\subsection{World-Model-Based Policy Learning}
\label{sec:wm-formulation}

Optimizing the CAV policy directly on \(\mathcal{M}\) requires repeated
simulator interaction.  We instead learn a latent transition model of the
traffic dynamics from collected transitions, and optimize the policy primarily
inside the learned model.  The segment traffic field is encoded into a latent
traffic representation \(Z_t=f_\phi(X_t,G)\), and the joint CAV action
\(a_t\) is projected onto the same segment graph as a planned pressure field
\(\Psi_t^{\mathrm{CAV}}\).
The learned transition then predicts the next latent traffic state under the
action and the exogenous conditions:
\begin{equation}
  \begin{array}{rcl}
  \hat Z_{t+1}
  &=&
  F_\phi(
  Z_t; \Psi_t^{\mathrm{CAV}},D_t,H_t^{\mathrm{HDV}}
  ),
  \end{array}
\end{equation}
Imagined rollouts generated by
\(F_\phi\) and the vehicle-level actor supply the transitions for actor-critic
updates, while simulator episodes remain the source of model fitting and final
policy evaluation.

UrbanDreamer instantiates this formulation with four modules.  The Flow Matching
Dynamics Model learns the transition \(F_\phi\): a traffic encoder maps
\(X_t\) to \(Z_t\), a decoder grounds the latent state in measurable
traffic attributes, an action encoder aggregates the sampled route decisions
into the planned pressure \(\Psi_t^{\mathrm{CAV}}\), and a flow-matching
model predicts the next latent state.  The HDV Response Module supplies the
episode-level estimate \(\hat H^{\mathrm{HDV}}\) from preceding episodes.
The Actor-Critic Learner optimizes the route actor \(\pi_\theta\) in
imagined rollouts with per-vehicle and global critics.

Together, these modules form the following training loop.  Simulator episodes populate a replay
buffer that trains the world model and the response predictor.  Replay states
initialize imagined rollouts, in which the actor samples routes, the world
model advances the latent traffic state, and a vehicle wrapper advances
individual vehicles; the imagined rewards and value targets then update the
actor and both critics.  Saved actors are finally evaluated in the simulator.
% 中文注释：直接在 MDP 上优化策略需要反复仿真。我们改为从采集的 transitions 学习 latent transition model，并在模型内优化策略：交通场编码为 \(Z_t=f_\phi(X_t,G)\),joint CAV action 投影为 planned pressure \(\Psi_t^{\mathrm{CAV}}=\Pi_G(\mathbf a_t,\mathcal{I}_t)\),learned transition 在动作和外生条件下预测 \(\hat Z_{t+1}=F_\phi(Z_t;\Psi_t^{\mathrm{CAV}},D_t,H_t^{\mathrm{HDV}},c_t)\)。想象 rollout 提供 actor-critic 更新所需的 transitions，仿真 episode 仍用于模型拟合和最终策略评估。
