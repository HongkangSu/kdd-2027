\subsection{Actor-Critic Learner}

This subsection realizes the microscopic level of
Section~\ref{sec:wm-formulation} and its two reward channels: each imagined
step produces a per-vehicle travel-time reward and a network-level global
reward, two centralized critics estimate the corresponding returns, and the
actor is updated from their combined advantage.  The per-vehicle signal is
the primary actor-learning signal, while the global critic supplies an
auxiliary aggregate signal.

\subsubsection{Vehicle-Level Imagination}

Imagined rollouts start from a replay state: we sample a replay episode and
a decision epoch \(t_{\mathrm{start}}\), encode
\(Z_{t_{\mathrm{start}}}=f_\phi(X_{t_{\mathrm{start}}},G)\), and initialize
each vehicle's current segment, destination, route suffix, and arrival
status from the same epoch.  At each imagined epoch, the actor selects a
route and the decoder recovers the segment speed \(\widehat v_{t,u}\).  The
deterministic vehicle wrapper of Eq.~\ref{eq:micro-advance} advances each
vehicle along its route by consuming the decoded travel times in route
order, while the learned transition predicts \(Z_{t+1}\); because both
consume the same decoded readout, vehicle positions and the segment traffic
field evolve from a single prediction rather than two independent state
estimates.  Departing vehicles are added, arrived vehicles are removed, and
the remaining CAVs form \(\mathcal{I}_{t+1}^{\mathrm{im}}\), which alone
contribute actor samples, rewards, and bootstrap values.

\subsubsection{Imagined Rewards}

For the route selected by CAV \(i\), let \(P_{i,t}^{\mathrm{rem}}\) denote
its suffix road segments.  The per-vehicle reward charges the imagined
travel time accumulated before the next decision or arrival:
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{rcl}
\widehat T_{i,t}^{\mathrm{rem}}
&=&
\displaystyle
\sum_{u\in P_{i,t}^{\mathrm{rem}}}
\widehat\tau_{t+1}(u),\\[0.35em]
\delta_{i,t}
&=&
\min(\Delta,\widehat T_{i,t}^{\mathrm{rem}}),\\
r_{i,t}^{\mathrm{veh}}
&=&
-\delta_{i,t}/\kappa_r,
\end{array}
\end{equation}
where \(\kappa_r\) controls the reward scale.  The reward saturates at
\(-\Delta/\kappa_r\) beyond one decision interval, but the return stays
action-dependent: faster routes make the wrapper remove the vehicle from
the active set earlier, ending the accumulation, and the final interval is
charged by the actual remaining time, so the per-vehicle return sums to the
negative imagined travel time.

The global reward charges each decision interval by the travel time
accumulated by all vehicles present in the network:
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{rcl}
r_t^{\mathrm{global}}
&=&
\displaystyle
-\kappa_g\,
\frac{N_{\mathrm{active}}(t)\,\Delta t}{N_{\mathrm{veh}}},
\end{array}
\end{equation}
where \(N_{\mathrm{veh}}\) is the episode vehicle population and
\(\kappa_g\) controls the reward scale.  On real transitions,
\(N_{\mathrm{active}}(t)\) is read from the simulator state; in imagined
rollouts it is summed from the decoded count field,
\(N_{\mathrm{active}}(t)=\sum_{u\in N}\widehat n_{t,u}\) with
\(\widehat n_{t,u}=\kappa(u)\hat c_{t,u}\).  Since
\(\sum_t N_{\mathrm{active}}(t)\Delta t\) is the total travel time
accumulated over an episode, the sum of \(r_t^{\mathrm{global}}\) equals
\(-\kappa_g\) times the objective of Definition~7.  Both rewards are
computed from decoded readouts rather than learned.
% 中文注释：per-vehicle reward 由 \(\delta_{i,t}=\min(\Delta,\widehat T_{i,t}^{\mathrm{rem}})\)、\(r_{i,t}^{\mathrm{veh}}=-\delta_{i,t}/\kappa_r\) 给出；global reward 的求和等于 \(-\kappa_g\) 倍 Definition~7 目标。两种 reward 都由 decoded readouts 计算。

\subsubsection{Critics}

The per-vehicle critic encodes each observation with a parameter-shared MLP
\(f_\eta^{\mathrm{enc}}\) and applies multi-head self-attention within the
active CAV set:
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{rcl}
e_{i,t} &=& f_\eta^{\mathrm{enc}}(o_{i,t}),\\
(\widetilde e_{i,t})_{i\in\mathcal{I}_t}
&=&
\mathrm{Attention}_\eta((e_{i,t})_{i\in\mathcal{I}_t}),\\
v_{i,t}^{\mathrm{veh}}
&=&
w_v^\top\widetilde e_{i,t}+b_v.
\end{array}
\end{equation}
The attention lets each value estimate account for simultaneous CAV
decisions and route overlap while remaining a per-vehicle estimate of the
future return.  The critic is trained by lambda returns linked by vehicle
identity, with bootstrapping stopped once the vehicle leaves the active set
(Appendix~\ref{app:math-details}).

The auxiliary global critic reuses the pooled graph representation
\(\bar z_t\):
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{rcl}
v_t^{\mathrm{global}}
&=&
V_\omega^{\mathrm{global}}
(\bar z_t).
\end{array}
\end{equation}
The planned CAV pressure conditions only the world-model transition and
never enters a value baseline, so \(v_t^{\mathrm{global}}\) is an
action-independent estimate of the network-level state value, capturing
aggregate congestion effects that no single CAV observation contains.  It is
trained from the epoch-level lambda return
(Appendix~\ref{app:math-details}), and its detached residual enters every
active CAV's actor advantage below.
% 中文注释：per-vehicle critic：shared MLP 编码 + active set 内 self-attention，按 vehicle identity 连接 lambda returns。global critic 读 \(\bar z_t\)，action-independent，其 detached residual 进入 actor advantage。

\subsubsection{Route Actor and Policy Update}

The actor scores every candidate route from a route representation
\(\varphi_{i,t,k}\), obtained by mean pooling the segment latents along the
route, and a shared context \(g_t\) containing the pooled graph latent, the
normalized decision time, and the active-CAV fraction
(Appendix~\ref{app:math-details}).  The scores are normalized into a
categorical distribution over the valid candidates:
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{rcl}
\pi_\theta(k\mid o_{i,t})
&=&
\mathrm{Softmax}_{k:m_{i,t,k}=1}
\!\left(f_\theta^{\mathrm{route}}(\varphi_{i,t,k},g_t)\right).
\end{array}
\end{equation}
Candidates with \(m_{i,t,k}=0\) are excluded from the softmax, and invalid
slots are masked out when fewer than \(K\) candidates exist.  Parameter
sharing across CAVs and candidate slots keeps the policy applicable to a
variable number of active vehicles.

For policy optimization, let \(\pi_{\mathrm{old}}\) denote the behavior
policy that produced \(a_{i,t}\).  The detached per-vehicle and global
advantages and their actor combination are
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{rcl}
A_{i,t}^{\mathrm{veh}}
&=&
\mathrm{sg}\!\left(
G_{i,t}^{\lambda}-v_{i,t}^{\mathrm{veh}}
\right),\\
A_t^{\mathrm{global}}
&=&
\mathrm{sg}\!\left(
G_t^{\mathrm{global}}-v_t^{\mathrm{global}}
\right),\\
A_{i,t}^{\mathrm{actor}}
&=&
A_{i,t}^{\mathrm{veh}}+\alpha_g A_t^{\mathrm{global}},
\end{array}
\end{equation}
where \(G_{i,t}^{\lambda}\) and \(G_t^{\mathrm{global}}\) are the
lambda-return targets of the two critics, \(\mathrm{sg}\) stops gradients
through the return targets, and \(\alpha_g\) weights the global credit
shared by CAV actions from the same imagined epoch, so policy improvement
internalizes network-wide congestion while retaining vehicle-specific
credit.  The actor is optimized with the standard clipped PPO surrogate
with entropy regularization (Appendix~\ref{app:math-details}); the global
critic thus affects the actor through \(A_t^{\mathrm{global}}\) rather than
only through a critic loss.
% 中文注释：actor 对候选做 masked softmax；advantage 组合 \(A^{\mathrm{actor}}=A^{\mathrm{veh}}+\alpha_gA^{\mathrm{global}}\)，标准 PPO clip + entropy。global critic 通过 \(A^{\mathrm{global}}\) 直接进入 actor objective。
